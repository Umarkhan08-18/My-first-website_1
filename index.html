Every formula is developed and manufactured in our own Karachi lab. We test each batch for stability and efficacy across diverse Pakistani skin tones and climates. Our ingredient percentages are printed on the label — no hidden actives, no filler claims.

We work with dermatologists during development and publish results timelines on every product page so you know exactly when to expect change.

How To Use

☀️ Morning (AM)
1 Cleanse face with a gentle cleanser and pat dry
2 Apply 3–4 drops to face and neck, avoiding eye area
3 Follow with moisturiser to lock in
4 Always finish with SPF 30+ sunscreen
🌙 Evening (PM)
1 Double cleanse to remove sunscreen and makeup
2 Apply 3–4 drops to clean skin
3 Follow with your night moisturiser
Storage: Keep in a cool, dark place. Refrigerate after opening for maximum potency. Use within 6 months of opening.

💡 Pro Tip: Layer this under your sunscreen in the morning — Vitamin C + SPF together provide significantly better protection against sun damage and pigmentation than either alone.

Caution: For external use only. Avoid direct contact with eyes. If irritation occurs, reduce frequency or discontinue use. Patch test on a small area before first use. Keep out of reach of children.
